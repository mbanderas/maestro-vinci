# Transferable patterns from the 2026-08-12 reference cohort

Use this file only after category matching in `fingerprint.json`. These are cross-source deductions, not a style recipe.

## Product proof should carry visual identity

Linear, Raycast, Stripe, and Shopify succeed when real product structure, code, or merchant evidence carries the story. Decoration reinforces proof; it does not substitute for proof.

Apply: expose the strongest real surface or mechanism early. Build composition around what the product does.

Reject: abstract gradients, glass, bento grids, or device frames that could survive unchanged after replacing the product.

## Information architecture can be the design

GOV.UK, the GOV.UK Design System, USWDS, Base Web, Radix, and Zendesk Garden show that dense systems become usable through stable navigation, explicit task labels, bounded reading measures, and predictable examples. Shadcn remains context-only: its taxonomy can be studied, but its default visual language is not a positive anchor.

Apply: choose hierarchy from user tasks and reader states before styling the shell.

Reject: visually sparse documentation that hides taxonomy, or dense navigation whose groups do not map to user intent.

## One authored constraint creates coherence

Linear uses dark product surfaces and one indigo accent. Stripe pairs editorial lightness with code and an owned color field. Cooper Hewitt uses bold condensed labels around collection imagery. Noema uses serif-led editorial pacing.

Apply: identify one constraint tied to product, content, or institution, then let it govern repeated decisions.

Reject: mixing recognizable signatures from multiple brands or treating a popular visual effect as the constraint.

## Content-specific variation needs a stable index

The Pudding, Pentagram, Cooper Hewitt, and Noema permit imagery, typography, or story treatment to vary because their indexes, labels, spacing logic, and navigation remain stable. Typewolf remains taxonomy metadata, not a positive composition anchor.

Apply: let content change art direction while preserving orientation and comparison.

Reject: arbitrary per-section styling without a stable editorial or navigational frame.

## Motion must reveal behavior

Motion.dev and Raycast make motion useful when it demonstrates timing, interaction, hierarchy, or a product state. Static screenshots remain insufficient evidence for the exact motion qualities.

Apply: state what motion teaches, provide reduced-motion behavior, and verify the rendered transition.

Reject: decorative movement that communicates no state or mechanism.

## Category diversity is a retrieval rule

More references do not create better taste when one category dominates. This cohort is strongest for developer products, documentation, public-service information architecture, editorial collections, and design systems.

Apply: retrieve by surface and industry, cap same-cluster sources, then select one structural anchor and at most two complementary anchors.

Reject: using the corpus mean as a universal house style.
