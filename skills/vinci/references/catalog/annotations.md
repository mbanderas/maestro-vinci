# Reference Engine 2026-08-12 annotations

## Evidence status

This catalog contains derived design observations from 24 public sites captured on 2026-08-12. Every viewport, mobile frame, and full-page contact sheet was inspected as pixels by Codex and a second independent visual judge. File hashes and dimensions were mechanically verified. No provider or API model analyzed the corpus.

The entries are advisory external anchors. They are not house voices, accessibility certifications, performance claims, conversion claims, or permission to clone protected identity. Raw screenshots remain in the operator-local, gitignored Design Reference Engine corpus and are not redistributed here.

## Collection adjudication

- Admit 20 sources for bounded positive retrieval. Keep `nextjs.org`, `ui.shadcn.com`, `vercel.com`, and `www.typewolf.com` as context-only or anti-template evidence.
- Exclude Garden versions/design routes, Linear `/homepage`, Stripe contact, and Pentagram work from positive retrieval. These captures were sparse, duplicated, obstructed, or incomplete.
- Do not promote any source into `references/positive/`; none is a Designer-owned voice.
- Cap retrieval at two matched sources per phase and three per task.
- Use at most one developer-tool or design-system source unless the target belongs to that category.
- Prefer transferable relationships: hierarchy, density, pacing, task structure, responsive change, information architecture, and proof sequencing.
- Exclude logos, brand marks, exact copy, proprietary product imagery, illustrations, code, and distinctive brand assets from transfer.

## Rejected capture sources

Six additional sources were excluded before training because captured pixels did not expose their primary design clearly:

- `1password.com`: consent layer obscured primary composition.
- `ourworldindata.org`: loading skeleton and consent layer obscured content.
- `wise.com`: consent layer obscured primary composition.
- `www.ikea.com`: large consent panel obscured every retained route.
- `www.nike.com`: modal consent layer obscured every retained route.
- `www.starlingbank.com`: consent modal obscured every retained route.

Rejection concerns capture quality, not the quality of the source website.

## Evidence boundary

The Design Reference Engine still records all 56 active screens as `pending`; no engine analysis, hostile review, quality tier, allowed-use decision, or model call ran. Designer admission is a separate, metadata-only, human-in-the-loop adjudication. It does not relabel engine state or establish world-class status.

## Residual collection gaps

Developer tools and design systems remain the dominant clusters. Cooper Hewitt and Noema add museum, collection, magazine, longform, and subscription-commerce evidence; Shopify adds ecommerce; Ncase and The Pudding add interactive editorial evidence. Consumer hardware, fashion, travel, health, entertainment, and consumer-finance references remain underrepresented and must not be inferred from this corpus.
